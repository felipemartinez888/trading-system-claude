### Implementation (branch `fix/m2-bounded-importer`, nothing committed)
- `src/tsclaude/marketdata/archive/importer.py`: `_run_klines` now fetches, merges, validates, stages
  (`staging.write_parts`) and reduces each month inside `one_month` under the existing semaphore; only
  small per-month summaries survive (an `array('q')` of times, zero-volume runs, source records,
  `PartInfo`s, a per-month `QACollector`, and the `Bar`s of cross-check sample months). Raw rows are
  unreachable when the month finishes. `ingested` is taken once before the gather. After the gather,
  results are sorted by month and folded in month order before the cross-checks, `build_report` and
  `_commit` (all unchanged). If any month fails, the sibling months are cancelled and drained before the
  error propagates, so nothing keeps fetching or writing into a closed staging area.
- `_bar_row` returns `(row, bar)`; `_validate` hands the `Bar`s to `zero_volume_runs`; `_bar_from_row` is
  deleted. Validation is one `Bar` construction per row (was two).
- Behavioural difference, deliberate: the per-month QA lists (`unit_notes`, `unavailable`, `malformed`, ...)
  were appended in task-completion order when `month_concurrency > 1`; they are now always in month order.
  At `month_concurrency=1` the output is byte-identical to the previous pipeline (test T2).
- `scripts/verify_live.py`: `build_report(..., required_s=None)`; monitored time is the union of the
  monitoring intervals clipped to the window; FAIL when `monitored < required_s - 60 s`

### Native crash seen while testing (not reproduced on purpose, not explained)
Two of five runs of the focused archive suite (stdout captured, `pytest -q`) ended with a Windows fatal
exception `0xC000001D` (illegal instruction), "Garbage-collecting", raised inside the explicit `gc.collect()`
of the T3 probe (`test_arc_import_memory.py`, legacy-pipeline probe). 9 other runs (3 more captured, 6 with `-s`,
plus the two full-suite runs and 6 runs of the memory module alone) passed. An illegal instruction raised in a
garbage collection is consistent with a native abort in an extension's GC traversal (the same family as the
PyO3 panic in `Bar.__init__`) but could equally be hardware/OS instability; no panic text was captured. It
occurred far below any memory limit (the memory-module peak is about 17 MB under tracemalloc), so it weakens
"memory pressure" as the sole explanation of the import crash. Not investigated further (out of AP-1 scope).
