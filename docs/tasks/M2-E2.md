# Task brief M2-E2 — Zero-volume classification and the narrow QA open exception

- **Status:** BRIEF ONLY. Implementation is **not authorized**; it needs separate owner approval (D-11 approved,
  D-13 approved in principle).
- **Owner:** Data Engineer. Reviewer: a fresh-context code review plus a provenance check of the QA outputs.
- **Branch:** `task/m2-e2` from the approved base. Uncommitted until reviewed.

## Goal
Make QA report the four zero-volume classes of ADR-0014 (Z1 genuine no-trade, Z2 outage filler, M missing, A
anomalous). Add the single ADR-0015 explained difference `leading_zero_trade_open` with all its safeguards. Raw
and derived bars, tolerances and the meaning of `cross_checks_ok` stay unchanged.

## Inputs
- ADR-0014, ADR-0015, ADR-0004; DECISIONS_PENDING D-11, D-13 and L-07(c).
- `docs/M2_DATASET_ACCEPTANCE.md`.
- Code: `src/tsclaude/marketdata/archive/qa.py` (`cross_check`, `zero_volume_runs`, `build_report`,
  `redact_holdout`), `marketdata/aggregate.py`, `marketdata/archive/importer.py` (`_cross_checks`).
- Evidence for the five cases: the ADR-0015 table (file SHA-256 values and line numbers).

## Deliverables
1. **Classification of every zero-volume minute.**
   - **Z1** requires `trades = 0`, all four volumes 0, O=H=L=C, and the open equal to the previous candle's close.
   - **A** covers everything else, including a zero-trade candle after a missing minute.
   - **Z2** applies to Z1 minutes inside a registered interruption (only once E-1's registry exists; until then
     it is reported as Z1 with an `in_registered_interval` note).
   - The QA summary shows counts per class. Class A rows are listed individually.
2. **Explained differences in `cross_check`.** A mismatch is `leading_zero_trade_open` only if **all** of these
   hold:
   - only `open` differs;
   - H, L and C are exactly equal;
   - volumes are within the existing tolerances;
   - trades are exactly equal;
   - the leading k ≥ 1 candles are Z1;
   - the derived open equals the first 1m open, the filler, and the close of the candle before the bucket;
   - the native open equals the first traded 1m open;
   - the bucket is complete;
   - the candle before the bucket is available (otherwise **fail closed**).
3. **QA summary:**
   - `cross_check_mismatches_explained` and per-reason counts;
   - `cross_checks_ok` true only with zero **unexplained** mismatches;
   - explained items keep full evidence (bucket, k, filler, first traded open, files, SHA-256, line numbers) and
     holdout redaction;
   - QA report version bump.
4. **Re-run instructions for the owner (O-7)** for B03, B10 and B12: an isolated re-import from the cached
   archives into new scratch folders. Read-only for existing data.

## Acceptance tests
- **The five real cases reproduce as explained,** from fixtures cut out of the cached archives (the minimal
  rows, recorded with their source SHA-256 and line numbers).
- **Mutations must make the exception fail:**
  - flip high, low or close;
  - change a volume beyond tolerance;
  - change trades;
  - make the derived open ≠ the filler;
  - make the leading candle non-flat;
  - make its price differ from the previous close;
  - remove the previous candle (month boundary).
- **Classification:**
  - Z1 versus A (non-flat; volume with zero trades; after a missing minute);
  - no candle is removed or added;
  - stored rows are identical before and after.
- **Visibility:**
  - `cross_checks_ok` stays false with any unexplained mismatch;
  - the explained count appears in the summary;
  - holdout redaction tests still pass.
- **Regression:** existing QA and importer tests pass (AP-1 bounded memory included).

## Constraints
- No change to stored bars, derived bars, tolerances, `contracts/` or the acceptance labels (D-15 deferred).
- No re-import of canonical data.
