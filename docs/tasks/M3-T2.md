# Task brief M3-T2 — Wire paper trading and backtests into the app and dashboard

- **Owner:** Platform & UI Engineer · **Effort:** medium · **Branch:** `task/m3-t2`

## Goal
The running app paper-trades live: confirmed signals flow through the risk engine into the
`PaperTradingService`, survive restarts, and the dashboard shows Paper and Backtests pages.

## Inputs
`CLAUDE.md`, `docs/DASHBOARD.md`, `src/tsclaude/sim/paper.py` (`PaperTradingService.open(config,
store, session_id=…)`, `status()`, `awaiting_missing`, `resume_after_downtime`, `reset_halt`),
`src/tsclaude/risk/`, `src/tsclaude/research/{runner,report}.py` and `/api/backtests`,
`src/tsclaude/runtime/supervisor.py`, decisions L-04, L-11.

## Deliverables
1. **Supervisor wiring:** on monitoring start, open/restore the paper service with the session's
   journal; feed it closed bars and market snapshots from `MarketDataService`; on restart run
   `resume_after_downtime` with bars from the live bar store / REST backfill; seed the risk market
   tracker's lookback from the live bar store at cold start. FAULTED / AWAITING_BACKFILL states are
   surfaced, never hidden. The demo detector stays WATCH-only and never feeds paper books.
2. **Operator actions (token-protected):** kill-switch reset (`reset_halt`, requires operator note),
   and L-11 "accept known gap" (operator note; logged event; exposed positions in that symbol closed
   at the next available open, flagged `spans_gap`). Request any new EventType from the Lead.
3. **API:** `/api/paper/books` (equity, drawdown, positions with unrealized P&L marked STALE when data
   is stale, orders, fills, ledger reconciliation status), `/api/paper/status`, `/api/paper/latency`
   (release-latency stats), `/api/signals` extended with lifecycle and shadow outcomes. Provenance
   SIMULATED for paper values, BACKTEST for backtests.
4. **Dashboard:** Paper workspace (Portfolio, Positions & Orders, Ledger, Books compare baseline vs
   shadow vs benchmark, kill switch control); Research › Backtests list and run detail (equity,
   drawdown, trade list, win rate with exact CI, p*, certified margin, n / n_eff / n_trials, worst
   trade, beyond-stop count, grade chip with reasons, manifest hashes). Empty states explain what is
   needed (e.g. "import history with scripts/import_history.ps1").
5. Status bar: paper service state (RUNNING / AWAITING_BACKFILL with missing symbols / FAULTED /
   HALTED).

## Tests
API integration with a fake paper service and with the real service on fake Binance; restart →
AWAITING_BACKFILL → resume; operator actions require token + note and are logged; vitest for the
new pages and grade/provenance rendering.

## Constraints
Write under `api/`, `runtime/`, `frontend/`, `scripts/`, tests. Do not modify `sim/`, `risk/`,
`research/`, `store/`, `marketdata/`, `contracts/` — request changes. CI is the real check
(`scripts/ci/wait_ci.sh <sha>`). Scratch prefix `m3t2_`. Commit early, Conventional Commits + session
trailer, push, short final report.
