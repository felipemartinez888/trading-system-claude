# Task brief M2-E1 — Exchange-interruption registry and no fills on zero-trade candles

- **Status:** BRIEF ONLY. Implementation is **not authorized**. It needs the O-4 evidence, then a separate owner
  approval. D-09 is approved in principle; D-10 is approved (the 2023-03-24 interval); D-12 is approved as a
  policy principle only.
- **Owners:** Sim & Risk Engineer (simulator), Quant Engineer (runner and reports). Causality Auditor review is
  mandatory.
- **Branch:** `task/m2-e1` from the approved base. Uncommitted until reviewed.

## Goal
Implement ADR-0013 (registry of exchange-wide interruptions, accepted in principle) and ADR-0014 rule 4 (no
simulated fills on zero-trade candles, a policy principle) **together**, so that backtest and paper (one
simulator, ADR-0005) never diverge. Raw data and general missing-data checks stay unchanged.

## Inputs
- ADR-0013, ADR-0014, ADR-0005, ADR-0009; RESEARCH_PROTOCOL §6; DECISIONS_PENDING D-09 … D-12 and L-04, L-11.
- `docs/devlog/2026-10-28-m2-decision-recommendations.md` §2–§3.
- The O-4 result (required: how live delivers zero-trade minutes).
- Code: `src/tsclaude/sim/book.py` (`_process_entry`, `_process_held`, `_close`, gap-fill), `sim/engine.py`
  (`BAR_GAP`), `research/runner.py` (integrity: `missing_minutes`, `missing_warmup_minutes`), `research/report.py`
  (flagged-share caps), `configs/backtest.yaml`.

## Deliverables
1. **`configs/exchange_interruptions.yaml`** (schema v1).
   - One entry: `binance-spot-2023-03-24`, spot, `[2023-03-24T11:28Z, 2023-03-24T14:00Z)`.
   - Signature evidence: symbols, identical boundaries, source SHA-256 and line 33880.
   - External sources, `approved_by: owner (D-10)`.
   - A loader that validates it (minute alignment, non-overlap, market, evidence fields) and fails closed on any
     error.
2. **Manifest:** `exchange_interruptions_hash` in every run manifest; `RUNNER_VERSION` bump.
3. **Runner integrity:**
   - Minutes inside a registered interval are excluded from `missing_minutes` and `missing_warmup_minutes` and
     reported as `exchange_downtime_minutes`, with the registry ids that intersect the range.
   - `max_missing_share` stays 0.0.
4. **Simulator** (`SIM_ENGINE_VERSION` bump), with semantics finalized after O-4:
   - no entry or exit fills on a candle with `trades == 0`, nor during a registered interval;
   - pending orders wait for the next candle with `trades > 0`, resolved with the existing gap-fill rule (the worse
     of open and level);
   - `valid_until` is never extended, so an expired order is cancelled;
   - an exposed position at the end of a registered interval closes at the first traded open (aligned with L-11)
     and is flagged `spans_exchange_downtime`;
   - strategies keep receiving the bars exactly as live would (no bridging; the absent minutes break history
     through the existing reset).
5. **Reports:**
   - the flag counts into the `spans_gap` share (existing 5 % cap gives grade C);
   - the count of fills deferred by the zero-trade rule;
   - the share of zero-trade minutes in the range;
   - the intervals in range.
6. **Paper:** the same simulator rules apply. Status and paper UI show deferred fills (read-only fields only).
7. **Devlog, metrics row, and a RESEARCH_PROTOCOL §6 wording update** for "data gaps in range" (the
   consequential text proposed in the policy devlog), applied only after approval.

## Acceptance tests (focused; no full suite)
- **Canaries:**
  - an **unregistered** gap still yields grade X (non-registered minute, single symbol);
  - a registered interval yields no X from those minutes and reports `exchange_downtime_minutes`;
  - an unregistered minute in the same run still yields X.
- **Registry:**
  - changing the registry changes the manifest hash;
  - an invalid registry fails closed (no run).
- **Execution:**
  - no fill on a zero-trade candle (limit, stop and market-at-open cases);
  - fill at the next traded candle with gap-fill;
  - `valid_until` expiry while waiting cancels the order;
  - an entry scheduled inside an interval executes at the first traded open if `valid_until` has not passed,
    and is cancelled if it has (one test for each case; the semantics are fixed after O-4);
  - an exposed position closes at the first traded open after the interval and is flagged.
- **Statistics:**
  - flagged trades are counted (none deleted), and the cap is applied above 5 %;
  - a synthetic run with no zero-trade candles has statistics identical to before (golden trades unchanged
    except the version);
  - backtest/paper parity on identical bars including zero-trade candles.
- **Regression:** existing look-ahead canaries, ledger property tests (known PARTIAL items excepted, unchanged),
  window-parity and causality-audit mutation tests still pass.
- **Mutations:** removing the no-fill check, or treating registered minutes as missing, must fail tests.

## Constraints
- No change to `contracts/` (Lead only) or to stored data.
- No change to the holdout guard.
- No new tolerance for missing data.
- Results recorded before E-1 must not be regraded silently: a recompute records the registry hash and the new
  versions.
